Turn scattered architecture notes into organised, structured project descriptions. ArchText is a simple, offline command-line writing assistant built for the architecture and design community.

Explore ArchText
What it does

From rough notes to clear ideas.

ArchText helps architecture students and young designers organise their project information and prepare clearer descriptions for portfolios and presentations.

01 / COUNT

Count your words

Measure words, characters, lines and paragraphs in your project notes.

02 / ORGANISE

Organise ideas

Sort rough notes into site context, design concept, objectives and architectural features.

03 / EXPORT

Export your work

Turn organised notes into a Markdown document that is easy to edit and reuse.

04 / CHECK

Check completeness

Review your notes for important project information using a keyword-based quality check.

Simple workflow

Designed to keep things simple.

ArchText works locally through your terminal. It does not require an internet connection or a paid AI service. You stay in control of your project information. 

$ archtext count oja-moferere.txt

$ archtext organize oja-moferere.txt

$ archtext check oja-moferere.txt

$ archtext export oja-moferere.txt
